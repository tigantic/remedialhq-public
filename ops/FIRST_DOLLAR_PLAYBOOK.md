# First-Dollar Playbook: Creator Signal Desk

## The validation goal

Sell a useful, manually delivered research pilot before funding autonomous publishing infrastructure. This is a 14-day demand test, not a forecast. No views, revenue, rankings, channel growth, virality, or other performance outcome is promised.

## Founding offer

Open **five founding slots at $99 each, paid up front**. The founding cohort is deliberately limited to GTA VI or closely related Rockstar coverage so the demand test measures one real audience instead of a collection of unrelated businesses. Each buyer receives:

- one source-linked change brief for a mutually agreed GTA VI or closely related Rockstar topic;
- three original content angles derived from the supported evidence;
- safe wording that separates confirmed facts, observed details, and unresolved claims;
- a list of claims that should be held or qualified; and
- one custom rumor check using public sources supplied by ReMediaLHQ or the buyer.

The pilot is research and editorial support. The buyer retains publishing judgment and responsibility. ReMediaLHQ does not transfer rights to third-party footage, artwork, logos, music, or source material, and the pilot is not legal, platform-policy, or intellectual-property clearance.

## Who is eligible

Use `docs/16_GTA_VI_PROSPECT_QUALIFICATION.md` as the mandatory cohort gate. Prioritize independent gaming creators, newsletters, sites, and podcasts with recent original GTA VI coverage, an active production cadence, a specific upcoming piece, and a stated public business route. Do not admit generic companies, gaming-adjacent vendors, or prospects selected only because they are hiring, funded, growing, or present in a sales database. Decline work that requires invented evidence, deceptive attribution, harassment, unlawful content, impersonation, undisclosed paid promotion, or use of material the buyer is not authorized to provide.

Use only public sources or buyer-provided material the buyer confirms it may share. Do not collect channel credentials, unpublished audience exports, payment-card data, or other sensitive customer data for this pilot.

Those two digests have different meanings and are never substituted for one another. `observed_at` must be a timezone-aware RFC 3339 timestamp. The validator rejects unknown fields, raw provider IDs, URLs, personal data, card data, secrets, non-live mode, the wrong base price, inconsistent tax or gross amounts, and files larger than 16 KiB. Released v1 evidence remains historical only and is rejected for new schema-5 purchase or refund events.

Optionally validate the file before recording the purchase:

```bash

  local-private/evidence/payment-captured.json \
  --order-id "${ORDER_ID}"
```

## Decision gates after 50 prospects

- **Continue and reprice:** at least two paid pilots, no material compliance or rights incident, and no more than one refund. Offer the next cohort only after reducing delivery time or testing a price closer to measured cost.
- **Revise the offer or target buyer:** at least five replies but fewer than two purchases. Use recorded objections to change one variable-buyer segment, scope, proof, or price-then run one new 50-prospect test.
- **Stop this motion:** fewer than five replies after all 50 contacts were genuinely personalized, or repeated feedback that the problem is not urgent. Do not compensate with more automation or higher outreach volume.
- **Pause immediately:** any dispute over source rights, disclosure, payment identity, refund handling, sensitive data, or a request to misrepresent evidence.

The pilot passes only when real paid demand and delivery economics are measured. Audience growth, ad eligibility, sponsorship inventory, and affiliate income remain later-stage possibilities.
