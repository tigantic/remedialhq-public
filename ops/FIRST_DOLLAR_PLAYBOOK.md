# First-Dollar Playbook: Creator Signal Desk

## The validation goal

Sell a useful, manually delivered research pilot before funding autonomous publishing infrastructure. This is a 14-day demand test, not a forecast. No views, revenue, rankings, channel growth, virality, or other performance outcome is promised.

## Founding offer

Open **five founding slots at $99 each, paid up front**. Each buyer receives:

- one source-linked change brief for a mutually agreed entertainment topic;
- three original content angles derived from the supported evidence;
- safe wording that separates confirmed facts, observed details, and unresolved claims;
- a list of claims that should be held or qualified; and
- one custom rumor check using public sources supplied by ReMediaLHQ or the buyer.

The pilot is research and editorial support. The buyer retains publishing judgment and responsibility. ReMediaLHQ does not transfer rights to third-party footage, artwork, logos, music, or source material, and the pilot is not legal, platform-policy, or intellectual-property clearance.

## Who is eligible

Prioritize independent gaming or entertainment creators, newsletters, and podcasts that publish original analysis and can name a specific upcoming piece. Decline work that requires invented evidence, deceptive attribution, harassment, unlawful content, impersonation, undisclosed paid promotion, or use of material the buyer is not authorized to provide.

Use only public sources or buyer-provided material the buyer confirms it may share. Do not collect channel credentials, unpublished audience exports, payment-card data, or other sensitive customer data for this pilot.

## Fourteen-day sequence

### Days 1–2: prove the deliverable

- Finish one source-linked sample that resembles the paid output without using a prospect's confidential material.
- Confirm the scope can be delivered in three owner hours or less.
- Create a one-page intake asking only for the creator's channel, audience, target topic, deadline, and relevant public links.

### Days 3–7: contact 50 qualified prospects

- Contact 10 prospects per day through their stated public business channel.
- Personalize each note with one specific reference to their recent work and one relevant sample insight.
- State the sender and offer plainly; use accurate subjects and claims; provide an easy way to decline future contact.
- Keep a suppression list and do not re-contact anyone who opts out.

Before any scaled commercial email, verify applicable anti-spam requirements, including sender identification, a valid postal address where required, and opt-out handling. This checklist is operational guidance, not legal advice.

### Days 4–12: sample and close

- Offer a small, reusable sample, not unpaid custom fulfillment, to qualified responders.
- Ask what deadline, evidence risk, and production bottleneck the creator wants to solve.
- Send the exact scope, price, delivery window, and refund terms after fit is confirmed. Obtain explicit written customer acceptance of that scope before sending the private payment link.
- Stop accepting founding orders after five captured founding purchases across the current and reconciled prior ledgers. A rejection, cancellation, or refund does not reopen a lifetime founding slot.

### Days 5–14: fulfill and learn

- Deliver each brief manually and run the same evidence and rights checks used for public content.
- Confirm a machine-safe scope reference, deadline, terms version, and claim IDs before checkout. After live payment is evidenced, record whether ReMediaLHQ accepts or rejects the order. Start work only after acceptance. Generate the brief from the ledger-verified order manifest so the delivered artifact has path-free provenance.
- Ask whether the brief saved time, changed wording, prevented an error, or created a usable angle. Record the answer as customer feedback, not as a guaranteed outcome.
- Log actual outreach, sampling, and fulfillment time; fees; refunds; objections; and any scope changes.

## Private operating record

Use `remedialhq pilot --help` to record the funnel and fulfillment lifecycle. The default ledger is `local-private/pilot-events.jsonl`. It is hash-chained, requests owner-only file permissions, enforces the five-purchase cap within one authoritative reconciled lineage, and accepts only structured facts, lowercase SHA-256 evidence digests, and opaque identifiers.

### Initialize schema 4 and reconcile prior slots

Initialization is mandatory. First create a durable owner-only reconciliation artifact that states how prior founding purchases were checked. Then initialize a new ledger:

```bash
remedialhq pilot init \
  --reconciliation-evidence local-private/evidence/pilot-slot-reconciliation.json
```

If a schema 1, 2, 3, or 4 ledger exists, preserve it unchanged and include it in the initialization command:

```bash
remedialhq pilot init \
  --reconciliation-evidence local-private/evidence/pilot-slot-reconciliation.json \
  --prior-ledger local-private/archive/pilot-events-v2.jsonl
```

The command verifies the prior hash chain, counts every prior `PURCHASED` event and inherited slot as consumed lifetime capacity, records the prior head digest even when no purchase was recorded, hashes the reconciliation artifact, and reports the remaining slots. Schema 1 through 3 ledgers are read-only migration evidence. A schema-4 ledger can be rolled into a fresh campaign ledger. Initialization refuses to overwrite an existing target ledger.

Designate exactly one successor ledger as authoritative, archive the predecessor read-only, and record that designation in the reconciliation artifact. The ledger enforces the cap within that lineage. It cannot prevent an operator from copying a local ledger, creating multiple successors from the same head, or continuing to append to a copied predecessor. File access controls and the reconciliation process must prevent those forks. Never copy events between ledger files.

If prior purchases cannot be reconciled from trustworthy evidence, do not accept another founding payment until the slot count is resolved.

### Import the qualified outreach plan before campaign day 3

Build the identity-bearing prospect list and the supporting evidence in the approved private system. The repository plan must contain only opaque prospect IDs, enumerated segments and channels, dates, booleans, and lowercase SHA-256 digests. Do not put names, email addresses, handles, URLs, message copy, notes, or evidence bodies in the plan or ledger. The machine-readable baseline contract is `config/outreach-plan.schema.json`. The local importer is authoritative and is stricter: it also enforces calendar-valid dates, canonical JSON integer tokens, cross-row campaign rules, and global evidence uniqueness.

The plan can be validated incrementally with 1 through 50 entries. Import is deliberately stricter and accepts only one complete cohort with all of these conditions:

- exactly 50 unique opaque prospect IDs and contiguous queue positions 1 through 50;
- exactly 10 planned contacts on each of campaign days 3 through 7;
- one eligible segment and one stated public business channel per prospect;
- explicit true qualification facts for original analysis, a specific upcoming piece, and a verified public business channel;
- distinct evidence digests for qualification, the recent-work reference, and the relevant sample insight for every prospect; and
- attested sender-identification, sending-domain, postal-address-review, and opt-out-process controls bound to a separate evidence digest.

## Model assumptions to replace with observed data

The repository model starts with 50 personalized prospects, a 20% reply rate, a 50% sample-request rate among replies, a 40% purchase rate among sample requesters, and a 5% refund rate. That produces an **expected two purchases and $198 booked revenue**, not a promise.

Under the illustrative 2.9% + $0.30 payment fee, eight outreach minutes per prospect, 0.5 sample hours per request, three fulfillment hours per non-refunded pilot, and a $35 owner-hour cost, the model yields about **$182 net cash before owner labor, 14.87 owner hours, and negative $339 economic contribution after owner labor**. ## Decision gates after 50 prospects

- **Continue and reprice:** at least two paid pilots, no material compliance or rights incident, and no more than one refund. Offer the next cohort only after reducing delivery time or testing a price closer to measured cost.
- **Revise the offer or target buyer:** at least five replies but fewer than two purchases. Use recorded objections to change one variable-buyer segment, scope, proof, or price-then run one new 50-prospect test.
- **Stop this motion:** fewer than five replies after all 50 contacts were genuinely personalized, or repeated feedback that the problem is not urgent. Do not compensate with more automation or higher outreach volume.
- **Pause immediately:** any dispute over source rights, disclosure, payment identity, refund handling, sensitive data, or a request to misrepresent evidence.

The pilot passes only when real paid demand and delivery economics are measured. Audience growth, ad eligibility, sponsorship inventory, and affiliate income remain later-stage possibilities.
