# GTA VI Creator Prospect Qualification

## Purpose

This is the mandatory audience gate for the 50-prospect Creator Signal Desk test in RMH-107. It keeps the cohort focused on people who already publish for a GTA VI or closely related Rockstar audience and can use a source-linked brief in current work.

Do not add a company merely because it is growing, hiring, funded, in gaming-adjacent technology, or reachable through a sales database. ReMediaLHQ is selling editorial research for GTA VI coverage. A prospect must have a direct, visible reason to care about that work.

## Hard pass gates

A prospect qualifies only when all six gates are supported by current public evidence:

1. **Audience fit:** the channel, publication, newsletter, or podcast regularly covers Grand Theft Auto, Rockstar Games, open-world games, or major console releases for an audience likely to follow GTA VI.
2. **Current GTA VI relevance:** at least one substantive GTA VI item was published in the previous 90 days, or an upcoming GTA VI piece is publicly announced. A passing item must contain original reporting, analysis, commentary, explanation, or production. A copied headline, unedited trailer repost, or generic social mention does not pass.
3. **Active production:** the prospect has published at least two original items in its primary format during the previous 60 days.
4. **Specific use case:** a researcher can state one plausible near-term piece where a source-linked change brief would save work or reduce claim risk. "They make gaming content" is not specific enough.
5. **Public business route:** the prospect publishes a business email, contact form, or business-purpose direct-message route. Personal contact data obtained from enrichment tools does not satisfy this gate.
6. **Service fit:** the expected request can be completed within the published Creator Signal Desk scope using public or buyer-authorized sources.

If any gate is unresolved, the prospect stays out of the cohort until the evidence is resolved. Do not substitute follower count, job postings, funding, or company size for a failed gate.

## Immediate exclusions

Exclude:

- fintech, software, staffing, consulting, or other businesses with no demonstrated GTA VI publishing audience;
- brands seeking promotion, sponsorship inventory, lead generation, or paid placement rather than editorial research;
- channels built primarily from leaks, stolen assets, copied footage, harassment, or unsupported claims;
- dormant channels and publications with no current production cadence;
- prospects whose only available route is a private or inferred personal address;
- prospects without a concrete GTA VI or Rockstar coverage use case; and
- any request that requires credentials, private audience exports, payment data, or unauthorized confidential material.

## Priority order

Fill the cohort in this order:

1. Independent YouTube creators publishing recurring GTA VI analysis.
2. Independent gaming sites or newsletters with a named GTA VI beat.
3. Gaming podcasts with recent GTA VI episodes and a continuing release-cycle format.
4. Console or open-world specialists with recent GTA VI coverage and an identifiable upcoming angle.

Large general-interest gaming outlets are lower priority when procurement, internal research staff, or long sales cycles make a $99 founding pilot implausible.

## Evidence packet for each prospect

Keep identity-bearing evidence only in the approved owner-private system. Before importing the privacy-minimized outreach plan, retain:

- the public channel or publication page;
- one qualifying GTA VI item from the previous 90 days;
- evidence of two original items in the previous 60 days;
- the stated public business contact route;
- a one-sentence upcoming-piece hypothesis;
- one specific reference to the prospect's recent work;
- one reusable sample insight that fits that work; and
- the UTC check time for every page used.

Hash the retained evidence artifacts and transfer only the opaque prospect ID, allowed enum values, dates, booleans, and required SHA-256 digests into the repository-defined outreach plan. Names, handles, URLs, contact details, notes, and message copy remain outside the repository and pilot ledger.

## Cohort quality check

Before import, inspect all 50 records as one cohort. The cohort passes only when:

- all 50 pass every hard gate;
- all 50 have a direct GTA VI or closely related Rockstar audience connection;
- no evidence artifact is reused across prospects;
- no personal contact data came from a paid enrichment or inferred-address tool;
- the five-day schedule remains exactly 10 planned contacts per day on campaign days 3 through 7; and
- the existing sender-identification, authenticated-domain, postal-address-review, suppression, and opt-out controls remain ready.

Passing this document's gate does not send outreach and does not complete RMH-107. It establishes that the people selected for the test could plausibly buy and use the service.
