# Autonomy Contract

## Autonomous authorities

After bootstrap credentials exist, the system may:

- retrieve allowlisted public sources;
- snapshot, hash, parse, and classify them;
- create and supersede claims without erasing history;
- generate original text, voice, diagrams, motion graphics, metadata, and pages;
- schedule publication within platform limits;
- run bounded title, thumbnail, timing, and format experiments;
- allocate capacity by expected contribution margin;
- publish corrections when claims change;
- pause any source, format, campaign, or platform when a circuit breaker fires.

## Hard invariants

1. No leaked or unlawfully obtained pre-release material.
2. No factual sentence without claim lineage.
3. No certainty laundering in titles, thumbnails, hooks, or captions.
4. No unlicensed commercial asset use.
5. No hidden sponsorship or affiliate relationship.
6. No silent correction.
7. No duplicate flooding.
8. No expansion of platform-token scope.
9. No self-modification of invariant policy.
10. No claim that revenue is guaranteed.

## Claim ladder

| State | Meaning | Public framing |
|---|---|---|
| `CONFIRMED` | Directly stated by an authoritative first party | “Rockstar states…” |
| `OBSERVED` | Visible/audible in official material but not explicitly explained | “The official presentation shows…” |
| `REPORTED` | An accountable third party owns the claim | “Outlet X reports…” |
| `INFERRED` | Reasoned interpretation from evidence | “This suggests…” |
| `PENDING` | Extracted but not reconciled | Not publishable as fact |
| `REJECTED` | False, prohibited, leaked, or unsupported | Debunking only, without reproducing prohibited material |

## Automatic-publication contract

A package may publish only when:

- every claim exists and is publishable;
- every asset is `OWNED`, `ORIGINAL_GENERATED`, `LICENSED_COMMERCIAL`, or `PUBLIC_DOMAIN`;
- rights risk is below threshold;
- recent semantic similarity is below threshold;
- title/body evidence alignment passes;
- required affiliate, sponsorship, synthetic-media, and independence disclosures are present;
- the platform adapter and account are healthy;
- platform and spend budgets are not exceeded;
- no circuit breaker is active.

Everything else is held automatically. Full autonomy includes the authority **not to publish**.

## Irreducibly human bootstrap authorities

- entity and beneficial-owner identity;
- domain registration and trademark clearance;
- platform account creation and acceptance of terms;
- tax and payout enrollment;
- OAuth consent and developer-app review;
- commercial licenses;
- sponsor contracts and other binding representations.

Once granted, these become machine-readable capabilities with scope, expiration, and revocation.
