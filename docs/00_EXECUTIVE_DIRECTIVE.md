# Executive Directive

## Mission

Capture the GTA VI attention cycle before launch, compound audience and search authority through launch, and preserve the mechanism afterward as a reusable autonomous event-media system.

The objective is not maximum content volume. It is maximum **qualified attention converted into durable owned assets**:

- returning audience;
- indexed answers;
- source-linked knowledge;
- email subscribers;
- monetizable viewing sessions;
- affiliate intent;
- sponsor inventory;
- reusable production infrastructure.

## Strategic thesis

GTA VI is the bootstrap corpus, not the terminal business.

```text
public change
  → verified claim
  → structured knowledge
  → original media package
  → distributed attention
  → owned audience
  → revenue + performance data
  → better allocation
```

A second vertical should require a new source registry, ontology, safety profile, brand layer, and monetization map-not a rewritten engine.

## Operating posture

1. **Evidence precedes language.** Every factual sentence resolves to claim IDs.
2. **Rights precede rendering.** Unclear rights produce an original replacement or a hold.
3. **Originality precedes scale.** Semantically repetitive output is a failure state.
4. **Utility precedes SEO.** A page exists because it answers a real question better.
5. **Speed follows structure.** Explicit uncertainty enables fast, honest publication.
6. **Corrections compound trust.** Corrections append; they never silently erase history.
7. **The machine fails closed.** Missing evidence, rights, disclosure, or credentials block publication.

## North-star metric

**Evidence-qualified contribution margin per production hour.**

Supporting metrics:

- qualified watch hours;
- returning viewers;
- search impressions and CTR;
- newsletter opt-ins per 1,000 sessions;
- affiliate revenue per intent session;
- sponsor revenue per 1,000 qualified views;
- source-to-publish latency;
- correction rate and correction latency;
- held-output rate by gate;
- content half-life;
- compute cost per published minute/page.

## Definition of success by February 1, 2027

- Enter YouTube’s Partner Program under the pre-February full-ad threshold, ideally by December 31, 2026.
- Own the most useful source-linked GTA VI claim ledger and launch-answer corpus we can build.
- Establish at least three independent revenue lanes.
- Instantiate one non-GTA vertical without rewriting the core.
- Record zero deliberate leaked-footage publications and zero unrecorded commercial-asset uses.
