# Content and Claim System

## Editorial promise

> Every claim has a state. Every state has evidence. Every correction remains visible.

## Franchises

### The Ledger
Fast, source-linked changes to officially established information.

### Mechanics Lab
Observed systems in official material, distinguished from final shipping behavior.

### Leonida Atlas
Structured people, places, organizations, routes, and relationships.

### Rumor Court
A disposition for high-demand claims without using the verdict as a pretext to reproduce leaks.

### Engine Room
Technical analysis of animation, rendering, simulation, materials, lighting, water, traversal, and world density.

### Launch Utility
Platforms, editions, settings, accessibility, mission help, locations, items, systems, and update tracking.

## One change, distinct outputs

| Surface | Distinct audience job |
|---|---|
| 8–12 minute YouTube | Narrative analysis and qualified watch time |
| 60–90 second vertical | Discovery and claim compression |
| Web ledger entry | Canonical source-linked record |
| Search article | Complete answer and internal relationships |
| X/Threads | Timely distribution and discussion |
| Instagram carousel | Visual hierarchy |
| Newsletter | Owned-audience retention |
| Structured-data update | Graph maintenance and future retrieval |

A proposition can travel across formats, but each derivative must add platform-specific utility.

## Quality score

```text
quality =
  0.24 evidence completeness
+ 0.18 audience utility
+ 0.16 originality
+ 0.12 narrative clarity
+ 0.10 source diversity
+ 0.08 shelf life
+ 0.06 visual distinctiveness
+ 0.06 disclosure completeness
- rights risk
- repetition
- unsupported certainty
```

Minimum automatic-publish score: `0.82`.

## Initial capacity

- three high-value long-form videos per week;
- one or two distinct vertical videos per day;
- five durable pages per week;
- one weekly ledger briefing;
- reactive output only after a verified change clears the opportunity threshold.

Underperforming formats lose capacity. No quota overrides quality.

## Source hierarchy

1. Rockstar/Take-Two first-party statements and product pages;
2. platform-holder pages and primary developer materials;
3. primary interviews and filings;
4. reputable, accountable reporting;
5. community observation that can be independently reproduced;
6. anonymous claims and leaks: quarantined and non-admissible as publication evidence.
