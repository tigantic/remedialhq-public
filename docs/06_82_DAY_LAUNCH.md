# The 82-Day Launch Campaign

**Day 0:** August 29, 2026
**Scheduled launch:** November 19, 2026

## Objective

Enter launch day with an already-classified channel, indexed site, trusted claim system, owned email list, and operational answer engine.

## Phase 0 - Authority bootstrap
### August 29–31 · Days 0–2

- clear/select public name, domain, and handles;
- create owner-controlled YouTube, TikTok, Meta, X, newsletter, analytics, and affiliate accounts;
- provision Google Cloud, OAuth clients, secrets, buckets, datasets, and identities;
- deploy site prototype and public methodology;
- produce the first three launch packages.

**Exit:** one identity across all surfaces; private dry-run passes end to end.

## Phase 1 - Prove the editorial product
### September 1–7 · Days 3–9

Publish:

- “what is actually official” long-form manifesto;
- 82-day countdown vertical;
- release-date/platform canonical page;
- claim-ladder explainer;
- first character and place dossiers;
- first Rumor Court disposition.

**Exit:** first 1,000 combined audience actions; baseline retention and CTR recorded.

## Phase 2 - Establish category ownership
### September 8–21 · Days 10–23

- three long-form videos weekly;
- one or two original verticals daily;
- five durable pages weekly;
- weekly ledger briefing;
- structured people/place/mechanics pages;
- email capture: “Only the changes that matter.”

**Exit:** 25 publishable claim nodes, 20 indexed pages, 30 vertical assets, and 8 long-form videos.

## Phase 3 - Build compounding surfaces
### September 22–October 12 · Days 24–44

- launch Leonida Atlas and mechanics graph;
- publish interactive evidence-state filter;
- build evergreen buying and launch-readiness pages;
- begin sponsor prospecting only after stable audience proof;
- localize winning content through separate quality gates.

**Exit:** newsletter conversion above 1.5% of qualified site sessions; non-brand search impressions in at least one cluster.

## Phase 4 - Conversion and qualification
### October 13–November 2 · Days 45–65

- concentrate on qualified watch hours and returning viewers;
- prepare launch playlists and internal-link structures;
- create answer templates without filling unknown facts;
- activate eligible affiliate programs;
- assemble sponsor inventory and rate card;
- drive YouTube qualification before January 31, 2027.

**Exit:** correction, publishing, and revenue telemetry pass a simulated 10× event load.

## Phase 5 - Launch staging
### November 3–18 · Days 66–81

- daily ledger delta;
- final platform/edition/download/accessibility facts when official;
- pre-render original graphics for likely query clusters;
- deploy launch command dashboard;
- load-test ingestion, rendering, cache, and queues;
- freeze speculative answer pages as drafts.

**Exit:** launch runbook rehearsed; scoped tokens and circuit breakers verified.

## Launch day - November 19 · Day 82

1. ingest owner-generated gameplay and approved official sources;
2. hash and segment source material;
3. detect entities, mechanics, missions, items, and locations;
4. reconcile against pre-launch claims;
5. answer the highest-demand verified questions first;
6. publish canonical records, then distinct derivatives;
7. update quickly when contradictory evidence appears.

## First 72 hours

Prioritize utility:

- getting started;
- settings and accessibility;
- mission blockers;
- money/economy systems;
- vehicle and item locations;
- wanted/police behavior;
- character switching;
- save/progression behavior;
- reproducible bugs and workarounds;
- pre-launch claims tested against the shipping build.

No fabricated “complete guide.” Coverage grows with verified evidence.

## Day 83 onward

Patches, updates, online features when official, PC state tracker without invented dates, databases, interactive tools, and a second vertical pilot.
