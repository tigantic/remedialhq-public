# Launch Day Command Center - November 19, 2026

## Objective

Own high-intent utility queries in the first 24 hours without sacrificing evidence, rights, or platform health.

## Pre-staged answer classes

- installation, preload, unlock-time, platform, and account questions;
- settings, performance, accessibility, controls, and save behavior;
- mission blockers and first-hour progression;
- locations, vehicles, weapons, businesses, collectibles, and systems;
- errors, crashes, patches, server status, and known workarounds;
- pre-launch claim validation: what shipped, changed, disappeared, or remained ambiguous.

## Autonomous loop

1. Ingest first-party release notes, support pages, and owner-generated play observations.
2. Cluster query demand every 15 minutes.
3. Bind candidate answers to shipping-build evidence.
4. Reject any answer that depends on copied leaks, unverified community claims, or unlicensed media.
5. Publish the smallest useful answer first; expand only after evidence and demand justify it.
6. Convert verified answers into video, web, social, newsletter, and structured-data variants.
7. Measure solve rate, return-to-search proxy, retention, affiliate intent, and contribution margin.
8. Append corrections rather than silently rewriting history.

## Capacity posture

- 1 canonical utility page per verified question cluster;
- up to 12 answer pages per day under the initial policy cap;
- up to 4 verticals and 2 long-form videos per day;
- merge duplicate intents rather than flooding variants;
- pause automatically after two material corrections in 24 hours or any ledger failure.

## Rights boundary after launch

Owner-recorded gameplay can enter the asset registry only with recorded ownership, platform-policy clearance, and a specific commercial-use scope. Third-party gameplay remains external unless licensed.
