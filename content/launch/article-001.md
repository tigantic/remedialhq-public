# GTA VI: the official state as of August 29, 2026

Rockstar currently lists **November 19, 2026** as Grand Theft Auto VI's scheduled launch date. The currently listed launch platforms are **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X|S**.

Official story material places the game in **Vice City** and the broader state of **Leonida**, centered on **Jason and Lucia**. Rockstar describes GTA VI as a **single-player experience**.

## Pre-orders, editions, and preload

Rockstar opened global pre-orders on **June 25, 2026** and currently lists two editions:

- Grand Theft Auto VI: Standard Edition
- Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition

Rockstar says pre-loading begins at local midnight on **November 12, 2026**. It also says the physical version contains a **digital download code inside the box** and does **not** include a disc.

## The Extended Look

Rockstar published its official Extended Look on **August 27, 2026**. The company says it was captured entirely from in-game footage on PlayStation 5. It appeared first on Netflix and later that day on Rockstar's official YouTube channel and GTA VI site.

“In-game footage” establishes the declared capture source. It does not, by itself, establish that every visible scene is player-controlled, unedited, final, or representative of every platform.

## What the current facts do not establish

The official state does not establish a PC release date, crossplay, final install size, final performance modes, a complete map size, online-mode launch timing, or every mechanic visible in a curated presentation.

Those remain separate claim records. They move only when qualifying evidence changes their state.
