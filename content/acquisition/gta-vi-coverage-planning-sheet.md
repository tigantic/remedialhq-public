# GTA VI Coverage Planning Sheet

Use this sheet to turn one GTA VI source change into a focused video, newsletter, podcast segment, or article. It is a reusable sample of the working method behind the Creator Signal Desk.

## 1. Define the publishing job

- **Audience:** Who is this for?
- **Format:** Video, Short, article, newsletter, podcast, or live segment?
- **Deadline:** When does the piece need to be locked?
- **Audience promise:** What will the viewer or reader understand by the end?
- **One question:** What exact question is this piece answering?

If the question cannot be written in one sentence, narrow the piece before researching.

## 2. Separate the evidence

Record every material point in one of these columns:

| Confirmed by an official source | Visible in official material | Still open |
|---|---|---|
| Dates, named platforms, published descriptions, direct announcements | Interface prompts, environments, actions, or other details visible in official footage | PC timing, online timing, final performance modes, map size, crossplay, or any other unannounced detail |

Do not move a visible detail into the confirmed column unless the source explicitly states the conclusion. Do not use the open column as permission to repeat a rumor.

## 3. Choose one angle

Pick one of these structures:

1. **What changed:** lead with the new official fact, then explain what it changes for the audience.
2. **What the footage shows:** isolate visible details, describe only what is on screen, and explain the limits of the observation.
3. **What remains unanswered:** list the highest-interest open questions without treating silence as evidence.
4. **Why the rollout matters:** analyze the release or presentation strategy using the published sequence and accountable sources.

Write the angle in this form:

```text
This piece shows [audience] what [new or overlooked point] means for [decision, expectation, or open question].
```

## 4. Draft the claim-safe spine

- **Lead:** What changed or why this matters now.
- **Evidence:** The strongest official source and exact support.
- **Observation:** What official footage visibly shows, if relevant.
- **Boundary:** What the available sources do not establish.
- **Takeaway:** The useful conclusion the evidence supports today.
- **Recheck:** Which live page must be checked immediately before publishing.

Useful wording:

- "Rockstar lists..." for a current official-page fact.
- "The presentation shows..." for a direct visual observation.
- "Rockstar has not announced..." for a genuine open question after checking the current source.
- "This suggests..." only when the inference and its limits are explained.

Avoid wording such as "confirmed" when the source only shows a prompt, object, animation, or edited sequence.

## 5. Final check before publishing

- Reopen every live source.
- Confirm dates, platforms, and product details have not changed.
- Keep source links in the description, show notes, or article.
- Remove any unsupported certainty introduced during scripting or editing.
- Confirm that every image, clip, logo, music track, and customer-provided asset is authorized for the planned use.
- Distinguish ReMediaLHQ or creator analysis from Rockstar's statements.
- Preserve the open questions instead of filling them with guesses.
