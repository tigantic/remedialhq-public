Evidence before amplification

Editorial Standards

ReMediaLHQ is optimized for speed without removing source discipline.

Claim states

  • Confirmed: directly established by an authoritative first party.
  • Observed: visible in official material but not explicitly established as final behavior.
  • Reported: attributed to an accountable third party.
  • Inferred: original analysis that preserves uncertainty.
  • Pending: held until qualifying evidence exists.
  • Rejected: failed, contradicted, prohibited, or withdrawn while preserving history.

Source handling

Sources are recorded separately from commercial media rights. A public URL can support a factual citation without granting permission to reproduce its video, imagery, audio, or text.

Corrections

Material changes append to the claim history. ReMediaLHQ corrects the public item and preserves the prior state when practical.

Leaks and stolen material

Pre-release stolen builds and leaked footage are excluded from the production asset pipeline. Reporting about the existence or impact of a leak may proceed using original language and qualified sources.
