GTA VI · Official-state brief

The official state as of August 29, 2026.

A source-linked snapshot of the date, platforms, editions, preload, story, and latest official presentation. The unknowns stay intact.

By ReMediaLHQ Editorial DeskUpdated August 29, 202612 tracked claims

Current official date: As of this page’s August 29 check, Rockstar’s official GTA VI page lists November 19, 2026 as the scheduled launch date. Dates can change; the linked first-party page is the live authority.

Rockstar currently lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S as launch platforms. Its official story material places the game in Vice City and the broader state of Leonida, centered on Jason and Lucia. Rockstar describes GTA VI as a single-player experience.

Pre-orders, editions, and preload

Rockstar’s pre-order announcement says global pre-orders opened on June 25, 2026. The company currently lists two editions:

  • Grand Theft Auto VI: Standard Edition
  • Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition

Rockstar says pre-loading begins at local midnight on November 12, 2026. Its platform-and-editions support record also says the physical version contains a digital download code inside the box and does not include a disc.

The Extended Look

Rockstar published its official Extended Look on August 27, 2026. The company says it was captured entirely from in-game footage on PlayStation 5. Rockstar staged it first on Netflix, then published it later that day on its official YouTube channel and GTA VI site.

“In-game footage” establishes the company’s declared capture source. It does not, by itself, establish that every visible scene is player-controlled, unedited, final, or representative of every platform.

What the current facts do not establish

The official state does not establish a PC release date, crossplay, final install size, final performance modes, a complete map size, online-mode launch timing, or every mechanic visible in a curated presentation.

Those remain separate claim records. They move only when qualifying evidence changes their state.
