Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the ReMediaLHQ service handles information through remedialhq.com, ReMediaLHQ inquiries, and the Creator Signal Desk. The legal seller and any additional contact details required for a paid order will be provided in the written order confirmation and receipt before payment.

Information submitted to ReMediaLHQ

ReMediaLHQ may collect information you submit, such as your name, email address, message, creator or publication name, public channel links, requested topic, deadline, sponsor inquiry, correction request, cancellation request, or support request. Do not send passwords, full payment-card or bank credentials, private audience exports, sensitive personal information, or confidential assets unless ReMediaLHQ has expressly agreed in writing to receive them.

Standard technical records may include IP address, browser type, device information, referring page, pages viewed, event timestamps, security events, and cookie or consent choices.

Stripe Payments

After ReMediaLHQ confirms fit and scope, it may send a private Stripe-hosted Payment Link. Stripe provides checkout and payment processing to ReMediaLHQ. ReMediaLHQ is the service seller and remains responsible for fulfillment, service support, cancellation decisions, and approved refunds. Stripe is not the seller or provider of the research service.

Stripe collects and uses payment credentials, contact and billing information, transaction data, device and authentication information, fraud signals, and refund or chargeback information under its own terms and privacy notice. Depending on the checkout configuration and payment method, Stripe may share with ReMediaLHQ the customer's name and contact details, billing details, payment method type, purchase amount and date, order reference, payment status, and refund or dispute status. ReMediaLHQ does not ask customers to send full payment credentials by email.

ReMediaLHQ uses the information Stripe makes available only as reasonably needed to confirm payment, fulfill and support the order, process approved refunds, prevent fraud, maintain financial records, and meet applicable obligations. Review the Stripe Privacy Policy and the terms shown on the Stripe-hosted checkout before paying.

Use of information

ReMediaLHQ uses information to assess fit, confirm scope, communicate with customers, deliver and support the service, document order status, process eligible cancellation or refund requests, operate and secure the site, prevent abuse, maintain financial and editorial records, comply with applicable obligations, measure performance, and improve its content and products.

Other service providers

ReMediaLHQ may use hosting, analytics, email, newsletter, payment, advertising, affiliate, and security providers. Information is shared only as reasonably needed for those services, an order, security, compliance, or a request from the person concerned. Each provider processes information under its own terms, privacy notice, and applicable agreement.

Cookies and analytics

Analytics and measurement tools may use cookies or similar technologies after deployment. Available consent controls will be presented where required. A private Stripe-hosted checkout may use its own cookies or similar technologies under Stripe's notices.

Retention

ReMediaLHQ retains information for the period reasonably needed to answer an inquiry, provide and support the service, document scope and delivery, satisfy applicable financial and recordkeeping obligations, resolve disputes, preserve editorial records, prevent fraud, and protect system security. Stripe applies its own retention rules to information it controls.

Your requests

Send a ReMediaLHQ access, correction, deletion, or privacy request to support@remedialhq.com. For information Stripe controls, use the controls and contact routes in Stripe's privacy notice. A request to ReMediaLHQ does not by itself delete information Stripe must handle separately, and a request to Stripe does not by itself delete a separate ReMediaLHQ record.

Contact

Service contact: ReMediaLHQ at support@remedialhq.com. The legal seller and any additional contact details required for a paid order are provided privately before payment.
