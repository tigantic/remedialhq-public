Account and platform data

Data Deletion Requests

Send deletion requests to support@remedialhq.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.

Include

  • The email address or platform identity associated with the request.
  • The ReMediaLHQ service or integration involved.
  • The information or account connection to delete.

Processing

ReMediaLHQ will verify the request when appropriate, revoke applicable access tokens, delete or de-identify eligible records, and confirm completion. Records required for security, fraud prevention, editorial integrity, legal compliance, or accounting may be retained for the applicable period.
